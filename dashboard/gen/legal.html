Terms of service

Your account

You sign in with your Google account. You must be at least 13 years old, and at least 18 (or have a parent or guardian's permission) to pay for anything. Anything done with your account or your API keys counts as done by you, so keep your keys private and tell us right away if you think someone else has access.

Hawkes Gen is its own product. An account here is separate from accounts on hawkesgen.ai or Hawkes Studio, even though the same Google account signs in to all of them, and credits don't move between them.

Credits, plans and payment

  • Work is paid in credits. One credit is worth one US cent, and every job's price is shown before it runs.
  • You can buy credit packs, or subscribe to a monthly plan that adds credits each month you pay. Payments go through Stripe, in US dollars; your bank may add its own fees.
  • A plan renews every month until you cancel. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep the plan until the end of the month you paid for, and you keep any credits you already have.
  • Credits don't expire while your account is open. They have no cash value and can't be moved to another account.
  • When a job starts, its price is held from your balance. If the job fails or you cancel it, the credits come back automatically.
  • New accounts may get free starter credits. We can change or stop free credits at any time.
  • We may change prices for future jobs and plans. A change never touches credits you already have, and a plan's new price starts at a renewal, after we tell you.

Your own AI accounts

Some tools use a language model. For those, you connect your own account with the provider (for example your own Anthropic API key, or your own Claude subscription). Your use of that account is between you and the provider: they bill you, and their terms apply. We store what you give us encrypted, use it only for your own jobs, and never show it back except its last few characters. We never run your jobs on our own AI accounts.

What you make

As between you and us, what you make is yours, along with the prompts and files you upload. You can use it for any lawful purpose, including commercial games and products. You give us only the permissions we need to run the service: to store your files, process them, and show them back to you. We don't use your prompts, uploads or results to train models, and we don't show them to other customers.

Generated work can resemble existing work, and the law on who owns AI-generated content differs by country and is still changing. It's your job to check that what you publish doesn't infringe anyone's rights.

What you can't do

  • Make or upload anything illegal, including sexual content involving minors.
  • Make content meant to deceive people about a real person: deepfakes, impersonation, or a real person's likeness or voice without their permission.
  • Upload material you don't have the rights to use.
  • Harass, threaten, or promote violence against people.
  • Attack, overload, scrape or reverse-engineer the service, get around its limits or billing, or resell access without our written permission.

We may refuse a job, remove content, or suspend an account that breaks these rules. If we close your account for any other reason, we'll refund purchased credits you haven't used.

The service

We work hard to keep Hawkes Gen running and our tools working, but the service is provided "as is". Results vary, and we can't promise a particular output, uptime, or that a tool stays available. We may change, add or remove tools.

To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect or consequential losses, and our total liability to you for anything to do with the service is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.

Ending

You can stop using Hawkes Gen and ask us to close your account at any time. We may change these terms; if a change matters, we'll tell you on the site or by email before it takes effect. Using the service after that means you accept the new terms.

Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Account: from Google, your email address, your name and Google's id for your account. We never see your Google password.
  • Your work: the prompts, settings and files you send, and what we make for you.
  • Billing: what you bought, your plan and your credit history. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Your AI accounts: the keys or sign-ins you connect, stored encrypted.
  • Usage: sign-in times, the jobs you run, and basic request logs (IP address, browser) kept for security and troubleshooting.

Cookies

We set a cookie to keep you signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. The video editor keeps your timeline in your own browser's storage.

How we use it

To run your jobs, keep your account and library, take payments, prevent abuse, answer you when you contact us, and tell you about changes to the service. We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

To make your work, we send each job (its prompt and the input files it needs) to the companies that run parts of it. Each handles it only to provide that service:

  • Google Cloud: the servers and file storage that run the service (United States).
  • Cloudflare: the network in front of the service.
  • RunPod and Modal: the GPU servers that make images and video (United States).
  • Anthropic, and other AI providers you connect: for tools that use a language model, on your own account.
  • ElevenLabs: some voice and sound tools.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Google: sign-in.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people from harm.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, library and credit history while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your files and personal data within 30 days. We keep billing records as long as tax and accounting law require.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account at any time by writing to us. Where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) may give you further rights, and we honor them.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). API keys are stored only as hashes, your AI accounts' keys are encrypted, and each customer's work is kept separate from every other customer's. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you.

Refunds

  • Failed and cancelled jobs: refunded to your credit balance automatically, right away.
  • Credit packs: within 14 days of a purchase, we refund it in full if you haven't spent any of that pack's credits.
  • Plans: cancel any time to stop the next renewal. Within 14 days of a monthly charge, we refund it in full if you haven't spent any of that month's plan credits.
  • A result you're unhappy with: generation is unpredictable, so finished jobs aren't refunded as a rule. If a tool clearly didn't do what it says, tell us and we'll make it right, usually with credits.
  • Charged in error, or twice: tell us and we'll refund it.

Card refunds go back through Stripe and usually reach your account in 5 to 10 business days. Write to us from the email on your account.
